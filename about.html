Corporate profile

Experience and innovation

Three decades after our founding, we had established ourselves as a quality manufacturer of minerals and speciality chemicals for the food, beverage, cosmetic and pharmaceutical industries. Our products are accepted worldwide and exported to more than 40 countries.

Commitment

All procedures meet stringent FSSC 22000 and cGMP standards, and products are manufactured under a HACCP programme. Shanpar is recognised as a GMP manufacturer and upgrades its facility regularly to maintain cGMP standards.

Competence

We support customers with cross-functional teams of technocrats. Innovation and flexibility drive our development work, and our experience with customised granules and powders turns your problems into value-added solutions.

Logistics

Our trained customer service team handles orders with professional accuracy, so they are executed just in time, every time.

Environment & safety

We use operating procedures that protect the environment while improving public health and safety, and we reduce our impact by conserving raw materials and energy and recycling waste.

Our vision

Grow as partners of multinationals.
