Process

General process flow

Every product follows the same twelve controlled stages, from raw material to dispatch. Nothing is packed until QC has approved the batch.

  1. 01

    Raw material

    Tested raw material and process water enter the reaction.

    + Process water
  2. 02

    Reaction

    Raw materials react to form the mineral salt in solution.

  3. 03

    Filtration

    The reaction solution is filtered and the residue rejected.

    Reject residue
  4. 04

    Evaporation

    The clear solution is concentrated; water is removed.

    Water rejects
  5. 05

    Crystallisation

    The salt crystallises out of the concentrated solution.

  6. 06

    Centrifuge

    Crystals are separated from the mother liquor.

  7. 07

    Drying

    Wet cake is dried in a fluid bed dryer.

  8. 08

    Milling

    Dried material is milled to size.

  9. 09

    Homogenisation

    The batch is blended to a uniform lot.

  10. 10

    Sifting

    Powder is sifted to the required mesh.

  11. 11

    Packing

    Packed after QC approval of the batch.

    QC approval
  12. 12

    Dispatch

    Released stock leaves for customers in India and abroad.
