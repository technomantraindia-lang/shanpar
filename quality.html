Food safety & quality policy

Safe, hazard-free food ingredients and nutrients.

B. N. PatelAuthorised signatory

  1. Systematic monitoring of work procedures, from receipt of incoming material to dispatch of finished goods.
  2. Eliminating physical, chemical and microbiological contamination, or reducing it to acceptable levels.
  3. Meeting all statutory and regulatory requirements for food safety and a quality culture.
  4. Managing internal and external communication.
  5. Keeping high standards of hygiene in plant, machinery and personnel.
  6. Building awareness of the food safety system among employees, suppliers and customers.
  7. Strict adherence to the food safety system and its continual improvement.

Laboratory

An independent QC section

Separate from the production area, staffed by qualified and trained analysts.

  • Segregated sections

    Instrumentation and chemical analysis, with a separate microbiology section on the first floor.

  • Calibration

    Calibration and qualification of instruments carried out in-house.

  • Written procedures

    For solvents, reagents, reference and working standards, cultures and media, and retain samples.

  • Stability studies

    For all process validation batches, then one batch of each product every year.

Validation master plan

Validated before it ships

The validation master plan sets out every validation activity. Requalification runs at defined intervals, and revalidation follows any change in equipment, critical process steps, specifications, active or primary packaging material.
